Juggernaut attack recordings from Dota 2 (© Valve and/or its licensors).

internal/sound/clips/miss-{1,2}.wav
Small blade whoosh recordings from Dota 2 (© Valve and/or its licensors).
miss-1.wav corresponds to Shared_small_blade_whoosh2.mp3.mpeg;
miss-2.wav corresponds to Shared_small_blade_whoosh3.mp3.mpeg.

The supplied MP3 recordings were converted to 22050 Hz stereo PCM WAV.
Playback lowers their level and applies a 3 ms fade at file boundaries.
These recordings are not licensed under MIT by this project. This notice
does not grant permission to redistribute them.
