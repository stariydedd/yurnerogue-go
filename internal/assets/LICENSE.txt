Audio (paths relative to the repository root)

internal/sound/clips/attack-{1,2,3}.wav
Juggernaut attack recordings from Dota 2 (© Valve and/or its licensors).
Supplied source files: Juggernaut_attack{1,2,3}.mp3.mpeg.

internal/sound/clips/miss-{1,2}.wav
Small blade whoosh recordings from Dota 2 (© Valve and/or its licensors).
miss-1.wav corresponds to Shared_small_blade_whoosh2.mp3.mpeg;
miss-2.wav corresponds to Shared_small_blade_whoosh3.mp3.mpeg.

internal/sound/clips/blade-dance.wav
Juggernaut Blade Dance recording from Dota 2 (© Valve and/or its licensors),
played on a Critical Strike hit at 80% on top of one of the attack recordings.
Supplied source file: Blade_Dance.mp3.mpeg. It starts at the recording's own
impact (0.205 s, 3 ms fade-in) so that impact coincides with the attack
recording's hit instead of sounding as a second hit; it ends at 1.6 s of the
source with a 0.25 s fade-out.

internal/sound/clips/blade-ring.wav
Large blade ring recording from Dota 2 (© Valve and/or its licensors), played
on a parried hit. Supplied source file: Shared_large_blade_ring1.mp3.mpeg,
copied from mono to both channels, with a 4 dB high shelf above 7 kHz and a
soft 90 ms impact mixed under it (the start of Juggernaut_attack1.mp3.mpeg,
low-passed at 1.2 kHz and lowered by 4 dB).

Both ability files are normalized to a -1.5 dB peak without compression or
limiting and raised in playback (+4.1 dB and +5.7 dB over the attack
recordings' level) to match the attack recordings' loudness.

The game plays audio at 44100 Hz. All supplied MP3s were converted directly to
44100 Hz stereo PCM WAV without compression or limiting. The attack files are
lowered by 1.5 dB and the miss files by 0.5 dB for headroom (the attack MP3s
peak above full scale); playback restores the same amount.
